# WonderPlay - Kids Play Zone & Indoor Play Area HTML Template

WonderPlay is a comprehensive, multipurpose HTML template designed specifically for indoor playgrounds, kids activity centers, adventure parks, and birthday celebration venues.

## Features
- **Public Website (17 Pages)**: 2 Distinct Home Pages (Play Zone & Birthday Events), Play Zones with Age Filtering, Zone Details, Birthday Packages & Theme Customizer, Party Details, Membership Plans, Safety & Hygiene Protocols, Blog, Pricing Matrix, Contact, Login, Register, 404, and Coming Soon.
- **Parent / Customer Dashboard (10 Pages)**: Session Booking Wizard with Multi-Child support, QR Check-in Pass, Membership Meter, Visit History, Invoices & Receipts, Profile & Children Management.
- **Admin Management Dashboard (15 Pages)**: Live Zone Occupancy Meters, SVG Analytics Charts (Revenue, Weekly Bookings, Popularity), Table Search/Filter, Booking Pipeline, Party Rooms, Packages, Payments & Settings.
- **Visual Design**: Warm cream background, deep purple (#5B3FD3), tangerine (#FF7A45), sunshine yellow (#FFD84D), mint (#52D6B5), Fredoka & Plus Jakarta Sans typography, sticker labels, offset playful shadows.
- **Dark Mode & RTL**: Built-in persistence for Light/Dark mode and LTR/RTL bidirectional layout.
- **No Emojis**: 100% professional SVG icons and playground vector assets.
