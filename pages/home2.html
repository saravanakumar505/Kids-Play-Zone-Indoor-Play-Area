Parent Play Guide

Before You Visit

Sign your electronic safety waiver online to skip the front desk queue and enter immediately on arrival.

What to Bring

Rubber grip socks for all children and adults. Reusable water bottles (refill stations available throughout).

Make The Most of It

Start in the low-sensory zones before heading to the high-energy Ninja and Adventure Arena ropes.
