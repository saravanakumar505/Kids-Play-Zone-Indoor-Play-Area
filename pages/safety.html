Medical-Grade Hygiene

15,000 Balls Washed & UV-C Sanitized Every Hour

Unlike traditional ball pits that are cleaned once a month, WonderPlay operates industrial-grade automated ball washing machinery. Balls are vacuumed, scrubbed with organic non-toxic disinfectant, exposed to hospital-grade UV-C light, and air dried.

Smart Security

RFID Matching Wristband Check-In

Every child and guardian receives encrypted matching RFID wristbands upon check-in. Our automated turnstile and exit security team verify that a child's band matches their guardian's band before anyone can exit the arena.

  • No child exits without matched guardian authorization
  • Digital photo ID verification on file in Parent Portal
  • Panic lock gate system on all perimeter emergency doors
